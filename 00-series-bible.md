# **Romeo, the Undying Hound — Series Bible**

*The fixed canon for the daily story. Edit anything here and every future chapter will follow it.*

## **Premise**

Romeo is a small red-and-white piebald dachshund (long body, short legs, enormous opinions) who has been alive for 3,112 years. Three millennia ago he ate a fallen spirit peach, awoke to sentience, and was granted a status screen by the System. He cultivated all the way to the peak of Tribulation Transcendence — the first beast ever to attempt the Ninth Heaven's Gate without a master's binding. On the threshold of Immortal Ascension, a silver chain reached down out of the clouds, a voice said "Sit," and the tribulation lightning struck him down.  
He woke at the bottom: Qi Gathering, Level 1, in the body of the young dog he was when he first awakened — with every memory, technique and grudge from 3,000 years intact. He is the oldest puppy in the world, and he intends to climb back up and find out who holds the leash.

## **The World: the Ninefold Ascent**

> * A xianxia world where the System arrived ten thousand years ago. Every sentient being has a status screen; the System narrates breakthroughs, skills, quests and tribulations in blue panels.  
> * Geography: the Ninefold Mountains rise in nine tiers toward the heavens. Mortal kingdoms and small sects sit in the foothills; great sects, ancient clans and beast domains occupy the higher tiers; the Heavenly Court sits above the clouds.  
> * Beasts can cultivate, but almost all are "bound" — contracted to a human master or sect. Unbound awakened beasts are hunted. This is the central injustice of the setting.  
> * Currency: spirit stones (low, mid, high, supreme grade). Pills, talismans, formations, and spirit herbs all exist and matter.

## **The System**

System messages appear in bold, in square brackets, one line per notice, for example:  
**\[SYSTEM NOTICE\]**  
**\[Breakthrough successful. Realm: Qi Gathering, Level 2.\]**  
The System is impartial, terse, occasionally dry. It almost never jokes. When it does, it matters.

## **Romeo**

> * **Look:** a small red-and-white piebald dachshund. Long, low body; short legs; long, soft, floppy ears; a reddish-brown head with a white blaze on the muzzle and chin; white chest, belly and paws; a red saddle across his back broken by a white band near the hips; a white tip on his tail; amber-hazel eyes and a liver-pink nose. A thin scar across his muzzle survives every remaking of his body. Signature expression: head tilted up, eyes wide, waiting (with infinite patience) for food.  
> * **Voice:** barks aloud; speaks mind-to-mind with spiritual voice (only to those he chooses). His inner voice is ancient, dry, patient and wry — an old master in a puppy's body.  
> * **Still a dog:** loves belly rubs and hates admitting it, cannot resist chasing spirit squirrels, buries things he values, tilts his head when confused, tail wags before his brain decides. These instincts are a running source of humor and sometimes of victory. He never becomes human or takes human form.  
> * **Dachshund nature:** bred to go to ground after badgers, so he is a born digger and tunneler and fearless against foes many times his size. Stairs, tall grass, high ledges and deep snow are real obstacles for him. He is stubborn to the bone, his bark is far louder than his body, and his long back is his physical weak point. Other beings constantly underestimate him because he is a sausage-shaped little dog; he lets them.  
> * **No hands:** every technique is done with jaws, paws, howl, nose, tail, or qi. Fights should feel canine (speed, scent, ambush, biting at the precise point) and specifically dachshund: going low, under guards, under doors, into burrows.  
> * **Immortality — \[Loyal Unto Heaven's End\]:** he cannot die of age. If killed, his body reforms at his Anchor (the grave of his first master, Old Man Shen, location currently unknown to the world) after seven days, losing one full realm of cultivation. Death is therefore survivable but costly — real stakes.  
> * **Grief:** he has outlived every person he ever loved. He is reluctant to bond again, and always does anyway.  
> * **Knowledge advantage:** he remembers lost techniques, hidden treasure sites, and the youth of today's ancient elders. He is short on power, never on knowledge.

### **Romeo's techniques (most are locked until his realm can support them)**

> * **Nine Scents Heavenly Tracking Sutra** — his core cultivation method; he refines qi through scent, tracing the "smell" of heaven and earth energy.  
> * **Heavenly Nose** — can smell lies, fear, poison, hidden treasure, qi deviations, and intent. Partially functional from Chapter 1\.  
> * **Buried Bone Storage** — spatial storage; whatever he buries he can retrieve anywhere. Capacity grows with realm.  
> * **Moon-Swallowing Howl** — sonic qi technique (locked).  
> * **Thousand Leash Breaker** — his signature technique for severing contracts and bindings (sealed; key to the main plot).

## **Cast**

> * **Ling Mei** (19) — outer disciple of the Azure Wolf Sect with "crippled" meridians, mocked as Rank Zero Mei. Stubborn, practical, quietly funny. She shared her last bun with a starving stray and became Romeo's bound companion. Romeo knows her meridians are not crippled but reversed — the mark of a nearly extinct physique. Their bond is the heart of the story: equals, not master and pet.  
> * **Old Turtle Bao** — a 10,000-year-old spirit turtle, Romeo's oldest friend and rival, sarcastic and slow. Not yet introduced; should appear in the first arc.  
> * **Zhao Peng** — arrogant outer-disciple bully, nephew of an Azure Wolf deacon. Petty early antagonist.  
> * **Elder Fang Wuji** — Azure Wolf Sect elder whose sect binds wolf spirit beasts. Wants Romeo as a bound beast. Mid-tier antagonist.  
> * **The Hound-Catchers** — agents of the Heavenly Leash who hunt unbound awakened beasts. Silver chains, masked faces.  
> * **The Heavenly Leash** — the hidden celestial authority that struck Romeo down. Its master's identity is the long-term mystery. Reveal slowly over many arcs.  
> * **Old Man Shen** (deceased, 3,000 years ago) — Romeo's first master, a poor herb gatherer. Appears in flashbacks. His grave is Romeo's Anchor.

## **Tone and style**

> * Xianxia \+ LitRPG with heart. Roughly 60% adventure and progression, 25% humor, 15% emotional weight.  
> * Third person, close on Romeo (occasional brief cutaways to other characters are fine).  
> * Humor comes from dog-logic colliding with ancient-master dignity. Never make Romeo stupid; make him a dog.  
> * Violence can be real but not gory. No sexual content. Romance, if any, belongs to side characters and stays light.

## **Rules for every chapter**

> * About 2,000 words (1,700–2,500). One clear event or turn per chapter. End on a hook.  
> * Earned progression: usually zero or one level per chapter. A chapter may grant several levels at once (up to about three in Qi Gathering, fewer in higher realms) only when a major, set-up catalyst earns it: a consumed treasure or spirit herb, a near-death ordeal, a deep insight, a refined or returned qi sea, or a climactic battle. Every multi-level jump must have a visible cost or aftermath (channel strain, exhaustion, unstable foundation, an enemy noticing). Gains slow as realms rise. Crossing a realm boundary always requires its tribulation. Realm breakthroughs roughly every 10–15 chapters.  
> * Arcs of about 20–30 chapters, each with its own goal and climax, all feeding the Heavenly Leash mystery.  
> * Continuity is sacred: honor the Ledger (stats, skills, items, injuries, locations, timeline). Never contradict a previous chapter.  
> * Include at least one System notification per chapter.  
> * Vary chapter shapes: fights, heists, tournaments, cultivation breakthroughs, quiet character moments, flashbacks to Old Man Shen, travel, mysteries.
