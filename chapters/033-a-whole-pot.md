# **Chapter 33: A Whole Pot**

Romeo's lip came up off his teeth before he had decided anything.

It was not a decision. It was three thousand years of being a dog who had found a rope tied round something he loved. His head went down to the black water in the crack, his jaws opened, and the thin bright thread singing up out of the lake was a finger's width from his teeth when Bao said, very quietly, ⟨Don't.⟩

**\[WARNING\]**  
**\[Binding tied with host's registered name.\]**  
**\[Severing by host will sever the name it is tied with.\]**

Romeo held still, jaws open, cold coming up off the water into his mouth like a blade.

*Sever the name,* he thought. *Not the binding. The name.*

⟨A knot tied with a name pulls both ways,⟩ said Bao. ⟨It holds me with you. Bite through it and you're biting through you.⟩

⟨It's a coat name. I have another.⟩

⟨It's not the coat I'm worried about,⟩ said Bao. ⟨It's what's in the pockets.⟩

Romeo breathed in again, and this time he made himself read what he smelled instead of just hating it. River water over stones in the sun. Reeds. An old man's sleeve, wet to the elbow. Straw. Red cotton thread. A day three thousand years ago when a young dog had gone into a river after a hat and come out with a name.

If he bit, the name would go. And the day would go with it, the way the far end of a rope goes when you cut the near one. He would still be Romeo to everyone who called him that. He would simply never again know why.

He shut his jaws. It took longer than it should have.

⟨Good,⟩ said Bao. ⟨I've been tied with worse. Last month I was tied with a wolf's dream. Leave it till we know whose hand is on the other end.⟩

---

Ling Mei was asleep before he had finished arguing with himself.

She lay on her side on the ice with her coat under her and her crooked arm across her face, and she was shaking again in small fits, the way a body shakes when it has gone past cold and doesn't know what else to do. Romeo looked at her and then at the long white shore and the long white mountain behind it, and knew that his back would not dig a second hole today, and that she could not walk a second night without sleep.

⟨Bao.⟩

⟨I know.⟩

The ice under her went milky.

It happened slowly, the way everything about Bao happened, a patch the size of a sleeping girl turning from black-clear to white and then to a soft, wet grey, like a stone that has lain all day in the sun. Warmth came up through it. Not much. The warmth of ten thousand years of mud a long way down, breathed up through a lake by something very tired that had decided to spend a little of what it had left.

Ling Mei stopped shaking. Her hand slid off her face. Romeo lay down along her back, chin on her hip, and listened to her breathing go long.

⟨Now,⟩ said Bao. ⟨Ask me your stupid questions, little brother. Before I sleep too.⟩

---

There were too many, and they had no time, so Romeo asked them the way you empty a bowl: fast, without tasting.

⟨The wolf's dream. A wolf that dreamed about snow.⟩

⟨A big blue one,⟩ said Bao. ⟨I didn't know his name. I knew his dreams. Running in the snow with something warm in front of him he couldn't see.⟩ A pause. ⟨That knot was slack when it was tied. Like a lead with nobody on the end of it. Two nights ago it went tight.⟩

Two nights ago, under Fang's window, a wolf had answered his mother.

⟨Can an answered name be taken back?⟩

⟨Can a pup be un-picked-up?⟩ said Bao. ⟨No. But a mouth can be made to let go of a scruff. That's a different question. Ask it later, when you're in front of the mouth.⟩

⟨The guard's hand. He promised to bring me up the passage.⟩

⟨Then it will,⟩ said Bao. ⟨A promise somebody's holding is a binding with no silver in it. The hand will do exactly what was promised and not one thing more.⟩ The slow voice turned this over. ⟨So be very sure where you want to be brought.⟩

Romeo filed that away; it was worth more than it sounded.

⟨On the dark night, four people want me in four places. The elder wants me in his room. The guard wants me at the bottom of the passage. The thing wants me at the top. The man in the gloves wants me chained to the stone.⟩

⟨You're long,⟩ said Bao. ⟨You're not that long.⟩

⟨Thank you.⟩

⟨You can't be in four places,⟩ said Bao. ⟨You can be in one place that three of them think is somewhere else. You've done it before. The monastery. The emperor with the bad teeth.⟩

⟨The emperor didn't have a turtle helping him.⟩

⟨The emperor,⟩ said Bao, ⟨didn't have a turtle at all. That was his problem.⟩

Then Romeo told him about the den. About the voice coming down the shaft in Shen's good-evening voice, with the whistle from the missing tooth, and about the one word at the end of it.

⟨It said *sit*,⟩ said Romeo. ⟨And there was no laugh after it.⟩

The lake did not move. Romeo felt, through his ribs, the turtle stop breathing.

⟨Where else,⟩ said Bao at last, ⟨have you heard it without the laugh?⟩

⟨You know where.⟩

⟨Say it anyway.⟩

⟨On the threshold. Out of the chain.⟩

Bao was silent so long that Romeo thought he had gone to sleep after all. When the old voice came back it was smaller, and very careful, the voice of something setting a cup down on a table it doesn't trust.

⟨Then I was right to be frightened,⟩ said Bao. ⟨And you're right to be going. I don't like that both of those are true. I'm too old to like two things at once.⟩

He would say nothing else about it. Romeo did not push. He knew that silence; it was the one that meant *later, and not out loud.*

---

⟨Last thing,⟩ said Romeo. ⟨The girl.⟩

⟨The girl is a long weight, very tired, lying on my warm patch with her mouth open.⟩

⟨Her meridians run backwards. When I put my qi through them, it comes home to me turned over. Denser. It made a level on its own, two nights ago, on a floor.⟩

Bao woke up all the way.

Romeo felt it: the lake under him drawing together, like a dozing old man who has heard the word *dumplings*.

⟨Backwards all the way through? Down and in?⟩

⟨All the way.⟩

⟨An Ebbtide,⟩ said Bao. ⟨Here. Carrying buckets.⟩ Something that might have been a laugh went through the ice. ⟨I haven't smelled one of those since the river had a different name. They used to stand them in the middle of a sect like a well, little brother, and everyone drew from them. Nobody ever thought to ask what the well wanted.⟩

⟨I'm asking.⟩

⟨You are,⟩ said Bao. ⟨That's why it works for you. Water goes where it's let.⟩ A long breath. ⟨Do you want to be level four when you walk up that passage?⟩

⟨No.⟩

⟨Then put your nose in my crack,⟩ said Bao. ⟨I'll give you a drink. You give it to her. She turns it over. You follow it home. All of it, this time. Not your little sip.⟩

Romeo looked at the sleeping girl.

*It's better when you ask,* she had said, in the dark of a hole under a boulder.

He put his nose against her ear. ⟨Ling Mei.⟩

"Mm."

⟨I need to do the soup again.⟩

"Do it, then," she said, without opening her eyes.

⟨Not a sip. A whole pot. Bao's. It will be cold, and it will be a lot, and it won't be comfortable.⟩

One dark eye opened. It looked at him for a while.

"Will it help?"

⟨Yes.⟩

"Will it help you or will it help *us*?"

He thought about lying, and could smell, quite clearly, how she would smell if he did.

⟨Me first. Then us.⟩

"Right," said Ling Mei, and closed the eye. "Don't wake me up for the boring parts."

---

He lay with his forepaws braced on the lip of the crack and put his muzzle down into the black water to the eyes.

The cold was so complete it was not cold any more. It was a sound. Then Bao's qi came up through it.

Romeo had tasted it before, through the shared conduit, a thread at a time, carried into Ling Mei like water carried in cupped paws. This was not a thread. This was the lake standing up. It came into his nose and his teeth and the roots of his long ears, heavy and slow and ancient, mud and moss and ten thousand summers of sitting still, and his little qi sea took one look at it and tried to run away.

*Don't hold it,* he told his body. *You can't. Pass it on.*

He opened the bond and let it go down.

Ling Mei made a sound in her sleep, a short hard breath, the sound of someone who has stepped into a river in the dark. Her tide took it. Romeo felt her reversed channels close round the flood and turn it, the way a millrace turns a stream, down when it wanted to go up, in when it wanted to go out, and somewhere in the middle of her, under the third rib on the left, it went over like a wave.

Then he followed it home.

He had done this on a floor with his own qi, scent by scent, a thread at a time. This was a river. He put his nose into it the only way he knew, *let it come to your nose*, and found the smell of it, mud and moss and girl, and walked it back out of her the wrong way round, and it came. It came into his channels turned over, dense as river stone and quiet as snow, and there was so much of it that for a moment there was nowhere in him that was not full.

His channels began to hurt.

Not the back. The back, for once, was the least of it. It was every thread of him that qi had ever run through, filling past where it had been made to fill, like a rope that has been asked to be a beam. He kept his nose in the water. He kept the long spine straight. Ling Mei breathed hard beside him and did not wake, and her good hand found the scruff of his neck and held on, not tight, the way you hold a dog in a crowd.

*More,* said Bao, somewhere very far down.

**\[Breakthrough successful. Realm: Qi Gathering, Level 6.\]**

The third wave came up the crack, and Romeo, who had been stubborn for three thousand years about everything, lifted his nose out of the water.

⟨Enough.⟩

⟨There's room,⟩ said Bao.

⟨There's room in me. There isn't in her.⟩ He could smell it: the fine, tired edges of her channels, warm and swollen like hands after buckets. ⟨And there isn't in you.⟩

The lake did not argue. That told him more than an argument would have.

Two levels in a morning. Bought with a turtle's strength and a girl's sleep and the ache now settling into every channel he had, the way cold settles into a house when the fire goes out. Three levels short of the gate. One night left after this one.

*Not enough,* he thought. *Better than four.*

⟨Thank you,⟩ he said.

Bao did not answer. Under the ice the great slow breathing had gone so far down that Romeo had to press his whole chest flat to find it.

---

It was then, with his chest flat to the ice and every channel humming, that he felt the thread.

His thread. The new one. It ran up out of the crack beside his nose and away under the ice toward the shore, and when the two new levels had gone into him it had hummed with them, the way a lead hums when the dog on the end of it starts to pull.

Something on the other end had felt it hum.

The thread went tight. Not hard. Gently, patiently, the way a fisherman lifts a line between two fingers to see whether anything is still on it.

And Romeo's left forepaw lifted off the ice, and reached forward, and set itself down one step nearer the shore.

He had not told it to.
